About the doctor

Three decades of drug-free, whole-person care in Agra.

Dr. Sunita Garg has led the Bliss Institute in Agra since 1990, combining naturopathy, acupuncture, therapeutic yoga and Traditional Chinese Medicine into a single, personal approach to health.

No two people arrive with the same story. Every patient begins with a careful TCM diagnosis and a conversation about their life, habits and circumstances. From that, Dr. Garg designs a programme of yoga, diet, meditation, counselling and bodywork for that one person.

The institute also hosts the Agra branch of the Sri Aurobindo Society, reflecting a belief that real healing reaches the mind and spirit as well as the body. Dr. Garg shares talks on conditions such as cervical spondylosis on her YouTube channel, Dr. Sunita Bliss.

Our principle: treat the person, not only the problem.

Our philosophy

Every disease is the outer sign of an inner disharmony.

Illness shows up when mind and body fall out of balance. Unless the inner disorder is healed, no outer cure can last. So at Bliss we don't fight one symptom at a time. We help the body heal itself.

Detox & cleansing

The body has its own healing mechanism. Cleansing clears what blocks it, so it can do its work.

Pranic energy through acupressure

Acupressure stimulates the organs and gives them pranic energy.

Balancing excess & deficit

Excess energy in one organ is moved to an organ that lacks it. Two diseases that look unrelated can often be treated with a single treatment.

Immunity for life

We treat the whole body, not one disease, and strengthen the immune system for the years ahead.

Fit forever

Learning to relax matters as much as learning to work out.

Along with diet and lifestyle guidance, every patient learns meditation and relaxation methods. This calm is what helps with stress-related diseases.

HypertensionHeart diseaseInsomniaAllergiesAutoimmune disorders

Through cleansing and detox, we have seen real improvement even in autoimmune disorders that modern medicine often calls untreatable.

Therapies & services

Every plan is written for one person.

We match each therapy to your diagnosis, your life situation and the depth of the problem, then adjust it as you progress.

Signature

Therapeutic Meridian Massage

Our specialised detox of mind and body. Meridian massage works along the energy channels mapped in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Each session is individualised after your TCM diagnosis, so pressure, sequence and focus change with what your body needs. A course is usually 10–15 sessions for chronic conditions, followed by periodic maintenance sessions.
